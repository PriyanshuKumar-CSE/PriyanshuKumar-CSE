### Hi, I'm Priyanshu Kumar

I'm a machine-learning engineer who builds computer-vision systems and tools that make ML easier to understand. I care about measurable results: my projects document how they work, what they assume, and where they fall short.

#### What I'm building

| Project | What it does | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**glasslearn**](https://github.com/PriyanshuKumar-CSE/glasslearn) | scikit-learn you can see through. Classic ML algorithms in plain numpy behind the scikit-learn API, with a narrated, step-by-step view of every `fit` inside your notebook. | Python, numpy, Jupyter |
| [**roadwidth-ai**](https://github.com/PriyanshuKumar-CSE/roadwidth-ai) | Measures road width from a single forward-facing video. It calibrates the camera from the footage, segments the road, rebuilds metric 3D geometry and cross-checks each measurement across three geometry models. | Python, PyTorch, Mask2Former, FastAPI |
| [**deepgaitv2**](https://github.com/PriyanshuKumar-CSE/deepgaitv2) | Identifies people by how they walk, with no face needed. A full pipeline from raw video to identity: YOLOv8 segmentation and tracking, silhouette processing and DeepGaitV2 embeddings. | Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, FastAPI, Streamlit |
| [**Govt_ai_voice_assistant**](https://github.com/PriyanshuKumar-CSE/Govt_ai_voice_assistant) | A phone assistant you can call to ask about government documents. It answers with retrieval-augmented generation over PDF sources. | Python, LangChain, Chroma, Twilio, FastAPI |
